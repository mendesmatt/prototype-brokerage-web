# Insurance Brokerage — Website

> Conversion-focused website prototype for insurance brokerages, featuring real-time quoting and end-to-end online policy purchase.

`HTML5` · `CSS3` · `JavaScript`

## 🇬🇧 English

**Segment:** Insurance & brokerage

Conversion-focused website prototype for insurance brokerages, featuring real-time quoting and end-to-end online policy purchase.

**Highlights**

- Instant multi-line quote calculator
- Catalogue across life, health, property and business lines
- Guided online purchase flow, from click to policy

**Tech:** HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript.

**Run it:** Open `index.html` in any modern browser — no build step, no dependencies to install.

_This is a design prototype: data is mocked in the front-end and there is no back-end._
